## Existing Pages Improved Instead of Duplicating URLs

- UK salary pages now include biweekly pay in the direct answer and pay-period table, addressing observed biweekly queries without creating separate biweekly salary URLs.
- UK and US hubs route to the new calculators, guide hub and Scotland cluster.
- The homepage routes to the guide hub, Scotland cluster and pay-difference calculator while preserving the original shallow architecture.

## Pages Rejected From Cohort

- Exhaustive annual/monthly/weekly/biweekly copies were rejected as insufficiently distinct.
- Full Scottish salary ladders were rejected; the cohort uses seven representative Scottish salary pages because the tax treatment is genuinely different.
- State-by-state expansion was rejected for Cohort 2 because the young site already has enough US representation and no GSC evidence justified a larger geography set.

## Internal Linking Changes

- Homepage links all calculation tools and the new guide/Scotland routes.
- UK hub links UK salary guides, Scotland, monthly/weekly and biweekly explanation routes.
- US hub links US salary guides, selected states and UK-vs-US take-home guidance.
- Guide hub links every new guide and comparison page.
- Salary pages link relevant tools, methodology and tax assumptions through existing sections.
