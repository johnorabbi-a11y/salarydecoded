FAQ

What is $150,000 after tax?

The V1 estimate is about $113,278 per year after federal tax, FICA and any selected state tax.

Does this include filing status choices?

V1 uses a single-filer standard deduction model. Future versions can add filing status controls.

Why can state results differ?

States may apply different income-tax systems, deductions and rates, while Texas and Florida do not have broad individual state income tax.
