FAQ

What does SalaryDecoded calculate?

It estimates gross salary, tax deductions, take-home pay, monthly pay, weekly pay and hourly equivalents for selected UK and US examples.

Is the calculator exact?

No. It is an educational estimate based on documented assumptions and simplified scenarios. Actual payroll can differ.

Why are there only selected salary pages?

V1 publishes only useful cohorts with clear independent intent, instead of generating every salary permutation.

SalaryDecoded provides educational salary estimates, not payroll, tax, legal or financial advice.
